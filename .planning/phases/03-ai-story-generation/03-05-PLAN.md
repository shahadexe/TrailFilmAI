1. **TripDetailHeader** — gain a three-dot menu (Lucide MoreVertical button + popover/menu) with a "Change tone & regenerate" option that opens the RegenerationDialog. The menu only appears when the trip has a story to replace, i.e. `generation_status` is `'completed'` or `'failed'`.
